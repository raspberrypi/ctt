Sharpen threshold

Finds the smallest sharpening threshold that does not amplify sensor noise: the sweep captures the Macbeth chart with sharpening disabled, then at each candidate threshold, and compares the spatial noise on the grey (bottom-row) patches. Frame the chart on the Capture tab first — static and steadily lit (a lightbox works well). Each point reloads the camera with a modified tuning, so the preview will blink and a run takes a few minutes.

Sharpening strength & limit

Tunes how hard edges are sharpened (strength) and the halo cap on strong edges (limit) by measuring a slanted edge in the processed output: MTF50 boost, MTF peak and the edge halos (over/undershoot) against a sharpening-off baseline. An ISO 12233 / eSFR chart (as used by the MTF tab) is the ideal target — its edges are moderate contrast (drives the strength choice) and its registration marks give the strong black/white edges the limit choice needs; keep the Macbeth chart in the scene too and grey-patch noise is recorded per point. Tune and apply the threshold first; all figures are ratios against the same scene, so keep it static and steadily lit.

Low gain is the worst case for halos — denoise barely intervenes and sharpening acts at full force — the opposite of the threshold sweep's high gain.
